Volunteer With Us

Join us in nurturing the future!

Your desire to join us in going the extra mile to nurture the future makes us super happy. Together, we can nurture a future we can be proud of.

Why Volunteer?

As a volunteer, you are at the heart of what we do. Your time and inputs push us to do more and become better for the children we serve. But more importantly, it gives you the opportunity to make a difference, expand your network, and hone your skills.

Volunteer Benefit You are eligible for a community service certificate or reference letter when you have proven to be an asset over a period of 6-10 months.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Do I need to buy the Happy Hands branded T-shirt?
As a first-time volunteer, you do. The T-shirt is ₦5,000. You have the option of choosing White, Orange, or Navy Blue.

2. What if I don't have money to contribute?
Yes! Your time is precious, and giving it is more than enough to make an impact.

Oops! Not a volunteer? Sponsor a child instead.
