Education Pillar

Scholars Program

Ensuring every child has access to basic education through merit and need-based scholarships.

Opening doors through education.

The Scholars Program is our flagship initiative designed to ensure that lack of funds never stops a child from learning. We provide comprehensive scholarships that cover tuition fees, extra-curricular activities, and continuous monitoring.

But we don't stop at paying school fees. Our scholars are enrolled in inclusive development programs designed to hone their social and emotional skills, ensuring they grow into well-rounded individuals ready to take on the world.

What the scholarship covers:

  • Full tuition fees
  • Extra-curricular activities support
  • Continuous academic monitoring
  • Inclusive personal development programs
