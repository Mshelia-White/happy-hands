Why Happy Hands

Happy hands are loving hearts translated into action.

Happy Hands is not just the name of an organization. It describes everyone who translates love, joy, and happiness from their hearts to their hands.

"Money is not the problem." — Founder's guiding belief

At Happy Hands, we provide children in underserved areas with equal opportunities to become agents of lasting change by creating an environment for their all-inclusive welfare. Since 2020, we have been bridging education and health gaps while creating development opportunities for children who deserve the right environment to bloom.

Mission

To incorporate a network of volunteers and partners passionate about helping children realize their full potential.

Vision

Creating a safe space for the African child while providing opportunities for inclusive growth.
