Health Pillar

Nourish Now

Food relief for children and families in need to combat malnutrition.

Fighting hunger, fueling potential.

Proper nutrition is foundational to a child's health and ability to learn. Hunger prevents children from focusing in school and leaves them vulnerable to illness.

The Nourish Now initiative provides temporary food relief through targeted campaigns. We ensure that vulnerable families have access to nutritious meals, especially during times of crisis or economic hardship.

Our Food Relief Programs:

  • Happy Boxes: Care packages of essential food items for families.
  • Eat 'n' Learn: Providing nutritious meals to students during school hours.
  • Food Bank (Coming Soon): A sustained community food reserve to support ongoing needs.
