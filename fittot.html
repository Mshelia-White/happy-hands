Health Pillar

FitTot

Quality healthcare access for vulnerable children.

Prevention, care, and wellness.

Every child deserves a healthy start in life, yet many preventable diseases continue to affect children in marginalized communities due to a lack of basic healthcare access.

The FitTot initiative is our upcoming healthcare drive designed to bridge this gap. We are building partnerships with medical professionals and HMOs to provide comprehensive, affordable care access to the children who need it most.

What to expect:

  • Medical outreach & screenings
  • HMO partnerships for enrolled children
  • Basic immunizations and deworming
