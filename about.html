Our Story

We exist to nurture changemakers.

A safe space for the African child, providing opportunities for inclusive growth and development.

Why Happy Hands

Happy hands are loving hearts translated into action.

Happy Hands is not just the name of an organization. It's a noun, a description befitting everyone who translates love, joy, and happiness from their hearts to their hands.

At Happy Hands, we provide children in underserved areas with equal opportunities to become agents of lasting change. We do this by creating an environment for their all-inclusive welfare. Since 2020, we have been bridging education and health gaps while creating development opportunities for children who deserve the right environment to bloom.

Is there more to our story?

Yes, you are more to our story than we can ever tell. For every time you give your time, money, or resources, you make it possible for the children we serve to see the world we are creating, truly, for them.

"Money is not the problem."

— Happiness (Founder), on creating avenues for access to quality education and health.
